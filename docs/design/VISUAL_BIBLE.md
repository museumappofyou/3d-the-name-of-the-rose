# Character visual bible

Character source descriptions, design choices and continuity constraints. These are design references; implementation and evidence decisions are documented in the [provenance records](../provenance/people.md).

## NEW CHARACTER — BASKERVILLE'S WILLIAM

**SOURCE FACTS:**

* Franciscan friar, over 50 years old.
* Exceptionally tall and thin; sharp, hawk-like nose; piercing eyes.
* Long freckled face, yellow hair with tufts extending from his ears, bushy yellow eyebrows.
* Agile and energetic demeanor; wears the coarse dark brown woolen habit of St. Francis with a knotted rope girdle.

**CANONICAL VISUAL DESIGN:**

* **age/build:** 50 years old, exceptionally tall and lean frame.
* **face/hair:** Piercing eyes, thin hawk-like nose, freckled long face, yellow hair tufts at ears, thick yellow eyebrows.
* **distinctive features:** Observant, keen expression, confident posture.
* **clothing/accessories:** Coarse dark brown woolen Franciscan habit, knotted rope belt, leather travel pouch, leather boots.
* **silhouette:** Extremely tall, slender, erect silhouette.

**VISUAL DESIGN DECISIONS:**

* Simple leather travel pouch holding small instruments.

**CONTINUITY LOCK:**

* Preserve hawk-like nose, yellow hair/eyebrows, tall/thin build, and dark brown Franciscan habit in every frame.

**IMAGE CHARACTER BLOCK:** `a tall slender 50-year-old Franciscan friar with a sharp hawk-like nose, piercing eyes, freckled long face, yellow hair with tufts extending from his ears, and bushy yellow eyebrows, wearing a coarse dark brown woolen Franciscan habit with a knotted rope girdle`

## NEW CHARACTER — SALVATORE

**SOURCE FACTS:**

* Monk with a face resembling hybrid monsters; head shaved not for penance but due to past eczema.
* Narrow forehead that would meet bushy eyebrows if hair were present; round eyes with small, highly active pupils.
* Nose is a flat bone with two dark, hairy nostrils. Wide, ugly mouth stretching slightly to the right with thin upper lip and thick hanging lower lip showing irregular, sharp black dog-like teeth.
* Speaks a chaotic "Babel" mixture of Latin, Provençal, Italian, and fragments of various dialects.
* Former member of the Pseudo-Apostles (Fra Dolcino's followers).

**CANONICAL VISUAL DESIGN:**

* **age/build:** Middle-aged, wild and slightly hunched posture.
* **face/hair:** Scabbed/shaved head, narrow forehead, round active eyes, dark flat nose with hairy nostrils, wide crooked mouth with thin upper lip and fleshy hanging lower lip revealing sharp black dog-like teeth.
* **distinctive features:** Monstrous, hybrid-animal facial features; intense, shifty expression.
* **clothing/accessories:** Tattered, patched monastic habit.
* **silhouette:** Slightly stooped, grotesque, wild silhouette.

**VISUAL DESIGN DECISIONS:**

* Worn, mismatched monastic robes showing his outcast past.

**CONTINUITY LOCK:**

* Keep the shaved/scabbed head, dark flat nose with hairy nostrils, crooked mouth with black wolfish teeth, and tattered habit across all frames.

**IMAGE CHARACTER BLOCK:** `a middle-aged grotesquely deformed monk with a shaved scabbed head, narrow forehead, flat nose with dark hairy nostrils, wide crooked mouth showing sharp black dog-like teeth, wearing tattered patched monastic robes`

## NEW CHARACTER — UBERTINO OF CASALE

**SOURCE FACTS:**

* Legendary leader of the Spiritual Franciscans, 68 years old.
* Hairless, smooth-shaven face; bald head ("kabak kafalı"); large sky-blue eyes; thin red mouth; pale skin stretched like a mummy over his skull ("sütte saklanmış bir mumyanınki gibi").
* Long, thin white fingers like a young girl dried by premature death.
* Wears Cluniac black Benedictine robes as a disguise to hide from Papal inquisitors.
* Highly emotional, weeping with mystical fervor, passionate apocalyptic demeanor.

**CANONICAL VISUAL DESIGN:**

* **age/build:** 68 years old, thin, frail, erect yet delicate frame.
* **face/hair:** Completely bald head, hairless smooth pale face with skin stretched tightly over skull like a white mummy, large striking sky-blue eyes, thin red lips.
* **distinctive features:** Intense, weeping mystical gaze; delicate white hands with long slender fingers.
* **clothing/accessories:** Black woolen Cluniac Benedictine monk's habit and cowl (disguise).
* **silhouette:** Frail, slender, ascetic silhouette.

**VISUAL DESIGN DECISIONS:**

* Simple wooden rosary wrapped around long pale fingers.

**CONTINUITY LOCK:**

* Preserve bald head, mummy-like pale skin, large sky-blue eyes, frail build, and black Cluniac robes in every frame.

**IMAGE CHARACTER BLOCK:** `a frail 68-year-old ascetic monk with a bald head, smooth pale mummy-like skin stretched tightly over his skull, large striking sky-blue eyes, thin red lips, wearing dark black Cluniac Benedictine robes`

## NEW CHARACTER — SEVERINUS OF SANKT WENDEL

**SOURCE FACTS:**

* Monastery herbalist, manager of the botanical garden, hospital, and baths.
* About the same age as William (around 50 years old).
* Highly knowledgeable in botany, medical treatises (*Theatrum Sanitatis*, *De virtutibus herbarum*, *De Plantis*), culinary herbs, and dangerous hallucinogenic plants.
* Cautious and protective about toxic herbs; friendly yet cautious when questioned about Adelmo, Berengar, and library secrets.

**CANONICAL VISUAL DESIGN:**

* **age/build:** 50 years old, medium height, lean scholarly build.
* **face/hair:** Weathered intellectual face, dark tonsured hair, calm observant eyes that grow cautious during questioning.
* **distinctive features:** Gentle smile, garden-stained fingers, attentive medicinal posture.
* **clothing/accessories:** Dark woolen Benedictine habit with a leather belt carrying dried herb pouches, small glass vials, and garden shears.
* **silhouette:** Slender, slightly stooped scholarly monastic silhouette.

**VISUAL DESIGN DECISIONS:**

* Practical leather belt with small botanical shears and dried plant pouches.

**CONTINUITY LOCK:**

* Maintain 50-year-old Benedictine herbalist appearance, tonsured dark hair, and leather herb pouch across all frames.

**IMAGE CHARACTER BLOCK:** `a 50-year-old Benedictine herbalist monk with a weathered intellectual face, dark tonsured hair, and calm observant eyes, wearing a dark woolen Benedictine habit with a practical leather belt carrying dried herb pouches and small shears`

## NEW CHARACTER — BURGOS'S JORGE

* **SOURCE FACTS:**
  * Blind elderly Spanish monk, oldest in the abbey after Alinardo; shrunken body with age ("gövdesi yaşlılığın ağırlığıyla küçülmüş").
  * Skin, face, and pupils snow-white ("derisi, yüzü ve gözbebekleri de kar gibi beyaz").
  * Blind eyes that stare forward fixedly as if still seeing; booming, majestic, prophetic voice.
  * Severe, unyielding expression against laughter, humor, and grotesque imagery (*babewyn*).
* **CANONICAL VISUAL DESIGN:**
  * **age/build:** Elderly man in his late 80s, short and shrunken frame bent with age.
  * **face/hair:** Snow-white skin, stark white hair and eyebrows, milky white blind pupils; stern, deeply lined unyielding face.
  * **distinctive features:** Blind white eyes staring forward fixedly; majestic prophetic presence despite shrunken posture.
  * **clothing/accessories:** Coarse dark Benedictine monk habit, wooden walking staff.
  * **silhouette:** Small, stooped elderly silhouette with raised head and white eyes.
* **CONTINUITY LOCK:**
  * Preserve shrunken elderly build, snow-white skin/hair, milky white blind eyes, and stern unyielding face in every frame.
* **IMAGE CHARACTER BLOCK:** `an elderly blind Benedictine monk in his late 80s with a shrunken body, snow-white skin, stark white hair, milky white blind eyes, and a stern unyielding expression, wearing a coarse dark Benedictine habit`
