# Visual realism audit — 29 September 2026

## Scope and method

I inspected the repository, served the app locally at `http://localhost:8001`, and reviewed the rendered world from the aerial view and normal walking height. Direct player views included the gate avenue/goat path, church portal, nave at noon and Matins, chapter entrance, scriptorium, library/Finis Africae/east hall, kitchen, refectory, cloister, gardens, cemetery, stable/farmyard/blood jar, south service range, smithy, skull chapel, ossuary, treasury, infirmary, baths, dormitory and hospice. I changed canonical hours using `?time=` and tested the lantern in the night interiors. Other minor subrooms were checked from the overview and source; their close range quality needs a final in-app acceptance walk. The user separately confirmed that the sounds are poor. I traced every source bank and researched recordings in the [dedicated audio audit](AUDIO_REALISM_AUDIT.md); the tool environment could not provide subjective audio listening. Browser screenshots were inspected during the audit but were not exported as image files.

Reproduce key views with `?place=stables&mode=walk&time=14`, `?place=cemetery&mode=walk&time=14`, `?place=scriptorium&mode=walk&time=15.2`, `?place=nave&mode=walk&time=2.6`, `?place=refectory&mode=walk&time=18.2`, `?place=skull-chapel&mode=walk&time=19.5`, `?place=ossuary&mode=walk&time=21`, `?place=treasury&mode=walk&time=8.4`, and `?place=infirmary&mode=walk&time=14`. Some URL visits briefly show the aerial/folio while the walk graph builds; wait for loading to finish. Press `F` for the lantern.

## What exists now

| System | Current implementation | Audit decision |
|---|---|---|
| Architecture | Procedural, batched geometry in `src/core/kit.js` and `src/world/{aedificium,aedLibrary,church,claustrum,outbuildings,gardens,terrain,trees}.js` | Keep footprint, room topology, massing and most wall/arcade meshes. Refine selected player-visible vaults, openings, roofs and transitions. |
| Asset loading | JPG textures, plan image, fonts; no `.gltf/.glb/.fbx`, `GLTFLoader`, `AnimationMixer` or skinned meshes | Add a documented asset conversion/loading path for replacement characters, animals and hero props. |
| Humans | Up to 46 visible, schedule and proximity managed by `src/world/people.js`; each person is rigid procedural submeshes from `people/figure.js`; no facial or cloth detail, no skeletal motion, no NPC collision | Preserve horarium and placement logic; replace rendered figures with a small coherent rigged cast and appropriate actions. |
| Animals | `src/world/animals.js` creates 59 geometric horses, mules, donkey, cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, chickens, dogs and cat; basic pivot idle/movement near camera | Preserve species, location intent and distance gating; replace animal meshes and use real idle/feeding/step clips. |
| Materials | Poly Haven albedo/normal/AO texture JPGs, world-space weathering/snow shader, separate stone/wood/ground material roles in `src/core/materials.js` | Preserve authored zoning and shader concept; fix map assignment (`aoMap` is also assigned to `roughnessMap`), scale, repetition and snow transitions. |
| Lighting/weather | ACES tone mapping, sky/canonical hour system, sun and limited flame lights, GTAO, procedural snow/smoke/fire | Keep system, tune by scene and hour. Lantern and flame need softer ranges and legible dark values. |
| Audio | `src/systems/audio.js` and submodules synthesize all footsteps, bells, animals, ambience and chant; spatial emitters and convolution zones exist; sound defaults off | Preserve routing, surface detection and zones. **Replace the audible synthetic source bank with real recordings and human chant**; see [audio audit](AUDIO_REALISM_AUDIT.md). |
| Navigation/performance | Walker capsule vs BVH collision, surface-coded triangles; frustum/distance culling; merged static batches; proximity updates for life; roughly 110 draw calls in overview in observed session | Preserve. Add light collision proxies for imported assets and asset-specific LOD/texture/animation budgets. |

The large-scale reconstruction is a success: the Aedificium reads as the site anchor, the church/cloister/service ranges are easy to orient in, the snow and mountains give a strong winter setting, and the schedule, map, place folios, walk/fly modes and pathways already support exploration. The audit targets close-view credibility.

## Critical findings

| Location / object | Observed problem and realism effect | Direction / technique | Book constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Throughout / footsteps, animals, bell, chant and work sounds | The user reports that current sound quality is terrible. Code inspection shows every recognizable cue is generated from oscillators, noise, filters and envelopes; no original field recording is loaded. Frequent player steps and story-defining chant/animal/bell cues can immediately reveal the world as synthetic. | **REPLACE AUDIO SOURCES, KEEP SPATIAL ENGINE.** Use the exact recorded-source shortlist and exclusions in the [audio audit](AUDIO_REALISM_AUDIT.md); record/commission actual Latin office chant if no shippable performance fits. Audition and mix in browser. | [Soundscape source](../../src/world/soundscape.js) cites extracted chant, stable, kitchen, library-wind and animal claims; preserve their place/hour intent. |
| Throughout, especially stable, scriptorium, church / all people | Heads, hands, hoods and robes read as simple solids. Identical silhouettes and rigid poses break the illusion of an inhabited community when approached. Seated bodies can be misaligned with furniture. | **REPLACE WITH REAL ASSET + ANIMATION.** Establish period-dressed, rigged monk, novice, worker, visitor and groom archetypes; vary height, face/hood/robe; retarget idle, walk, sit, pray, write, carry, tend. Maintain current timetable and population throttling. Verify feet and hands at each pose. | [People provenance](../provenance/people.md) records 60 monks, work groups, 30 scribes and service population; [church evidence](../research/book/CHURCH.md) describes choir occupancy and stalls. |
| Stable, folds, farmyard / every animal species | In the first stable view the horse is plainly a blocky construction; other species are built from spheres/cylinders and share the same artificial surface language. Movement is rigid. | **REPLACE WITH REAL ASSET + ANIMATION.** Use licensed rigged horses first, then cattle, pigs, hens, sheep/goats/donkey/mules/dogs/cat by visibility. Small breathing, head, feeding and pecking loops are enough. Keep Brunellus black and in his described stall. | [Stable evidence](../research/book/OTHER_BUILDINGS.md) includes mangers, metal-grated door, morning sun, first-left Brunellus; [animal provenance](../provenance/animals-dressing.md) distinguishes explicit from ambient livestock. |
| Ossuary and skull chapel / bone display | Ossuary lantern view showed huge triangular, close-camera geometry covering much of the screen and a blown-out wall. Skull chapel front reads as a flat graphic panel of repeated cartoon skulls and thin crossed lines. These are focal narrative objects. | **REBUILD + REAL ASSET + LIGHTING.** First diagnose offending geometry/camera collision in ossuary. Build coherent skull/bone niches with weathered human-bone meshes, distinct piles/rows and non-repeating scale; keep accessible passage. Rebalance lantern exposure. | [Church evidence](../research/book/CHURCH.md) and place folios describe bone arrangement, skull niche and passage to kitchen. |
| Cemetery / grave field | Rectangular dark raised boxes, repeated cross blocks and hard snow outlines look like a game grid; graves seem either floating or sharply sunk. | **REBUILD GEOMETRY + TEXTURE.** Vary grave height, age, orientation, chipped markers and a few eroded slabs, but make most graves subtle under shallow snow. Blend disturbed soil into snow. Keep path and oak. | [Cemetery evidence](../research/book/OTHER_BUILDINGS.md) says old weathered stones, newly raised stones, and in snow only some tombs still rose above ground. |
| Infirmary / long ward | Beds are black/white rectangular solids in a nearly empty stone tunnel. They read as abstract blocks rather than straw mattresses, bedding and patient care. | **REPLACE WITH REAL ASSET / GEOMETRY REFINEMENT + CLUTTER.** Bed frames, uneven linens, rolled blankets, bowls, jars, work table, shelves and instruments; living but restrained activity. | [Infirmary evidence](../research/book/OTHER_BUILDINGS.md) explicitly mentions shelves, bottles, jugs and jars. |
| Farmyard / blood jar tableau | The narrative jar is a huge smooth orange polygon, blood is a flat sharp red rectangle on the snow and nearby worker/pig are primitive; the story focal scene looks like a blockout. | **REBUILD focal prop and ground effect + REPLACE characters/animal.** Give large earthenware vat rim, interior, staining and nearby work tools; shape stained snow/soil organically with restrained gloss. Keep book placement in front of henhouses behind choir. | [Blood-jar folio](../../src/data/places.js) gives precise location and narrative role. |

## Low findings and investigation items

- **KEEP / minor polish:** Mountains, overall site silhouette, cloister arcades, winter color family, plan UI, hour and weather transitions, church massing, labyrinth topology, movement and collision framework. Add realism only where normal player sightlines reveal a break.
- **Investigate further:** The portal spawn briefly labels `Via arborum` while showing the church portal, likely a zone overlap. The smithy spawn faces a near wall/door; hospice spawn appears pressed into a wall so stone fills the screen. The ossuary's giant geometry may be a camera start/clipping defect rather than bad bone meshes throughout. Test whether roof snow appears inside apertures in weather variants.
- **Scale and integration:** Calibrate humans (~1.5–1.8 m) first, then horses (withers/head), cattle, doors, tables, benches, beds, graves and carts. Imported meshes require consistent meter units, feet planted to BVH ground, no hoof flotation, no wall/seat penetrations, no decorative collider blocking a walk path. NPCs currently do not collide with the walker; light capsules only for near characters are preferable to full mesh collision.
- **Testing lens:** Every correction should be reviewed at walking height at a calm daylight hour, a snow hour, dawn/dusk and a narratively important night hour. Check recognizability without opening the folio: stable, kitchen, scriptorium, church, infirmary, baths, cemetery, smithy and crypt should communicate purpose on sight and sound.
