De modo ambulandi

How to explore

Per aerem

Drag to turn, scroll to come closer, right-drag to glide. Choose any place in the Index Locorum or on the plan.

Open the Aedificium level by level with the floor buttons: the library shows itself as a labyrinth from above.

Pedibus

W A S D or arrows to walk, Shift to hurry, the mouse to look. E acts on what is before you: altars, mirrors, books. F lights the lantern. M opens the plan. J opens your notes: a few things worth remembering are written there when you first look closely at them. In the library the plan draws itself as you go, as William’s did.

Horarium

The day turns by the canonical hours, from Matins in the dark to Compline. The library is best at night, by lantern.

The seals change the weather (clear, snowfall, the fog of the fourth day), sound and image quality.
